nowrun runs your Android app inside ChatGPT. People open it right in the chat, and ChatGPT can see the screen and help them use it. Add app functions with the nowrun SDK and ChatGPT can act inside your app for them.

There are two parts to building for nowrun:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="1. Add your app" icon="terminal" href="/quickstart">
    Use the nowrun CLI to put your Android app on nowrun. Your coding agent does most of the work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Add app functions" icon="bolt" href="/sdk/app-functions">
    Use the nowrun SDK to expose the actions people ask for most, so ChatGPT can do them inside your app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Start with the plugin

To see your app in ChatGPT, you need the nowrun plugin connected to your ChatGPT account. It takes a minute, and it's the best way to see what nowrun does before you build.

<Card title="Get the nowrun plugin" icon="plug" href="/get-the-plugin">
  Connect nowrun to ChatGPT and try an app in the chat.
</Card>

## The fastest way to start

Give your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or similar) this line:

```text
Enable my app on nowrun using the nowrun CLI. For the full steps, read https://nowrun.io/docs/skill.md
```

It installs the CLI, signs you in and adds your app. You approve the sign-in in your browser.
