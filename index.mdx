nowrun lets you run real apps inside ChatGPT. Open an app in the chat, use it there, and ask the assistant for help with what's on screen. Nothing is installed on your phone or computer.

## For developers

Want to bring your own app, or let the assistant act inside it? There are two parts:

1. [Upload your app](/quickstart) to nowrun with the nowrun CLI.
2. [Add app functions](/sdk/app-functions) with the nowrun SDK.

Coding agents: start with [skill.md](https://nowrun.io/docs/skill.md).
