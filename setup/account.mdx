Creating your account is the only part you do by hand. After that, your coding agent takes over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [nowrun.io](https://nowrun.io) and create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your card">
    A $0 card check activates the free plan. You aren't charged, and you stay on the free plan until you choose to upgrade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the CLI in your browser">
    When your agent runs `nowrun login`, a browser tab opens. Approve it and the CLI is signed in. There's no token to copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can see your apps and manage your plan at [nowrun.io](https://nowrun.io).
