When your agent creates your app, it needs three things from you:

| Field | What it is | CLI flag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | One or two sentences on what your app does. | `--description` |
| **Pitch** | One line on why someone would use it. | `--pitch` |
| **Requests** | A few things people would ask for that your app handles. Add as many as you need. | `--intent` |

These help ChatGPT understand what your app does and when it fits what someone asks.

## Write requests the way people talk

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Vague">
    **Description:** The best cooking app!

    **Pitch:** Cooking made easy

    **Requests:** food, recipes, cooking
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clear">
    **Description:** Save recipes and turn them into a shopping list.

    **Pitch:** Go from recipe to shopping list in one step.

    **Requests:** "What can I make with chicken and rice?" "Add these ingredients to my list"
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Other fields

The CLI also needs a title, package name, category, kind (`app` or `game`), orientation and icon. Your agent can read the package name and icon from your APK with `--from-apk`, and list the categories with `nowrun app categories`.

To change any of these later, your agent runs `nowrun app update`.
