---

Once your app is created, your agent deploys your build:

```bash
nowrun deploy -f app-release.apk --wait
```

The CLI reads the version from your APK and refuses a build whose package name doesn't match your app, so you can't deploy to the wrong app by mistake. `--wait` blocks until the build is ready.

## Open it in ChatGPT

1. Make sure the [nowrun plugin](/get-the-plugin) is connected to your ChatGPT account.
2. Ask ChatGPT for your app by name.
3. Tap **Open**.

Your app starts out private, so only you can see and open it. It's already in your container, so there's no **Search and install** step.

## Check it works

- Ask the requests you wrote in [Describe your app](/deploying/describe-your-app) and see whether ChatGPT reaches for your app.
- Ask *what do I do now?* with your app open, and check ChatGPT's answer matches what's on screen.
- If you've added [app functions](/sdk/app-functions), ask for those actions and check they run.
