Once the [plugin is installed](/install-the-plugin), try these in a chat.

## Open an app

```text
Open Drape
```

The first time you open an app, tap **Search and install**, then **Open**. After that, you just tap **Open**.

Not sure which app you need? Describe it instead:

```text
Help me reorder paper towels
```

The assistant suggests apps you can install.

## Ask the assistant to do something

Drape is a sample app with app functions, so the assistant can act inside it:

```text
Put me in a beach look
```

```text
Make the shirt aqua
```

## Ask about the screen

In any app, the assistant can see what's on screen:

```text
What do I do now?
```

It tells you where to tap.

## Good to know

- On desktop, use fullscreen so the app stays beside the chat.
- Close an app with the **X** on the app.
- Only one app runs at a time. Opening another ends the current one.
