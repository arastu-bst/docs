nowrun runs real Android apps in the cloud and streams them into ChatGPT through the nowrun plugin. Nothing is installed on the user's phone or computer.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **nowrun plugin** | Connects ChatGPT to nowrun. Users add it once from **Plugins** in ChatGPT. |
| **Your nowrun container** | Where a user's apps run. Each user's container starts empty, and they choose what goes in it. |
| **nowrun CLI** | How you add your app to nowrun. Your coding agent runs it for you. |
| **nowrun SDK** | How you add app functions, so ChatGPT can act inside your app. |

## What ChatGPT can do with your app

**Open it.** A user asks for your app by name and it opens in the chat.

**See it.** While your app is open, ChatGPT can look at the screen and tell the user where to tap. Every app gets this, with no extra work.

**Use it.** If you add app functions with the nowrun SDK, ChatGPT can do things inside your app for the user, like adding an item to a list or changing a setting. This is the part that makes your app feel built for ChatGPT.

## From build to ChatGPT

1. **Your agent creates the app** on nowrun with the CLI, including a short description of what it does.
2. **Your agent deploys your APK.** nowrun gets it ready to run in the cloud.
3. **You open it in ChatGPT.** New apps are private, so only you can see and open yours. It's already in your container, so there's no install step.
