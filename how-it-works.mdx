nowrun runs Android apps in the cloud and streams them into ChatGPT through the nowrun plugin. Nothing is installed on the user's device.

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **nowrun plugin** | Connects a ChatGPT account to nowrun. Each person adds it once. |
| **nowrun container** | Where a person's apps run. It starts empty and they choose what goes in it. |
| **nowrun CLI** | Adds your app to nowrun. Your coding agent runs it. |
| **nowrun SDK** | Adds app functions to your app. |

## What the assistant can do with your app

| Level | What it means | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Someone asks for your app by name and it opens in the chat. | Your app on nowrun |
| **See** | The assistant reads the screen and tells the person where to tap. | Nothing extra |
| **Act** | The assistant runs actions inside your app, like adding an item to a list. | App functions |

## Visibility

New apps are private. Only the owner can see and open them. Your own app is already in your container, so it opens without an install step.
