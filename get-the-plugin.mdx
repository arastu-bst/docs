<Note>
  **For agents:** a person must do these steps in ChatGPT. Show them this page and wait.
</Note>

## Connect the plugin

1. In ChatGPT, open the menu and tap **Plugins**.
2. Search for **nowrun** and tap **+**.
3. Sign in to nowrun, or create an account.

You only do this once.

## Try an app

1. In a chat, type `Open Drape`.
2. The first time you open an app from an app store, tap **Search and install**, then **Open**. Next time, just tap **Open**.
3. Ask for something, like `Put me in a beach look`. Drape has app functions, so the assistant makes the change inside the app.

In apps without app functions, the assistant can still see the screen. Ask `What do I do now?` and it tells you where to tap.

## Good to know

- On desktop, use fullscreen so the app stays beside the chat.
- Close an app with the **X** on the app.
- Only one app runs at a time. Opening another ends the current one.
