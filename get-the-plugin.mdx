The nowrun plugin is how apps run inside ChatGPT. You add it once.

## Connect the plugin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plugins">
    In ChatGPT, open the menu and tap **Plugins**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add nowrun">
    Search for **nowrun** and tap **+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to nowrun when asked, or create an account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try an app

Ask ChatGPT for an app by name. Try Drape, a sample app built with the nowrun SDK:

```text
Open Drape
```

The first time you open an app from an app store, tap **Search and install**, then **Open**. After that, you just tap **Open**.

With Drape open, tell ChatGPT what you want, like *put me in a beach look* or *make the shirt aqua*. Drape exposes app functions, so ChatGPT makes the change inside the app.

Most apps don't have app functions yet. In those, ChatGPT can still see the screen. Ask *what do I do now?* and it tells you where to tap.

## Good to know

- **Use fullscreen on desktop.** The app sits beside the chat and stays in view while you talk.
- **Close an app with the X.** ChatGPT can't close it for you.
- **One app at a time.** Opening a new app ends the one that was running.
