You need a nowrun account and an Android build of your app (an `.apk`). Your coding agent can do the rest.

## Hand it to your agent

Give your coding agent this line:

```text
Enable my app on nowrun using the nowrun CLI. For the full steps, read https://nowrun.io/docs/skill.md
```

It installs the CLI, signs you in, creates your app and deploys your build. The steps below are what it does, so you know what to expect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [nowrun.io](https://nowrun.io) and verify a card. It's a $0 check, with no charge on the free plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the CLI">
    Your agent runs `pip install nowrun` and `nowrun login`. Your browser opens and you approve the sign-in. That's the only step you do yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your app">
    Your agent asks you for a short description, a one-line pitch and a few example requests your app handles. These help ChatGPT understand when your app fits what someone asks. See [Describe your app](/deploying/describe-your-app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy your build">
    Your agent creates the app and deploys your APK, then waits until it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it in ChatGPT">
    With the [nowrun plugin](/get-the-plugin) connected, ask ChatGPT for your app by name and tap **Open**. Your app is private, so only you can see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
