---
title: "Install the nowrun plugin"
sidebarTitle: "Install the plugin"
description: "Add the nowrun plugin to ChatGPT and sign in with your nowrun account."
---

1. In ChatGPT, open the menu and tap **Plugins**.
2. Search for **nowrun** and tap **+**.
3. Sign in with your nowrun account, or create one.

That's it. You only do this once.
